GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

How long does a print take?

It depends on size, layer height, infill, and complexity. The quote builder gives a planning estimate, and we confirm the final timing after reviewing your file.

What file types can I send?

STL and OBJ files are best for the most useful estimate. We also accept 3MF, GLB, GLTF, PLY, and FBX for an initial review.

What material do you offer?

PLA is our standard material. If you need ABS, PETG, resin, or another material, mention it in your request and we’ll let you know what is available.

Can you ship my finished print?

Yes. Include your location and deadline in the request so we can account for packaging and delivery.
